About me

I'm finishing a B.S. in Computer Science and Data Analytics at Rutgers (4.0 GPA, graduating December 2026). Most of what I build is generative AI that has to be trustworthy: agent pipelines that pull structured data out of messy documents, evaluation frameworks that grade LLM output, and human-in-the-loop checkpoints so that a person signs off on the calls that matter.

This summer I built exactly that at Bristol Myers Squibb, automating periodic reviews of validated systems in a regulated environment. Before that I was Head TA for 800+ Rutgers students, explaining data pipelines and databases to people who had never written a query.

Outside of work I follow the markets. Financial filings are one of my favorite test cases for AI: a lot of text, real consequences, and an unforgiving audience. That's why my most recent agent reads 10-Ks and 10-Qs.
